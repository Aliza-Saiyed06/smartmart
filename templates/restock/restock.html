Restock Recommendations

A simple rule based on past sales. No machine learning.

How the recommendation is calculated

  1. Average daily sales = units sold in the last {{ lookback_days }} days ÷ {{ lookback_days }}
  2. Recommended stock = average daily sales × {{ planning_days }} planning days (rounded up)
  3. Restock quantity = recommended stock − current stock (never below 0)
Example: Milk sells 12 per day. For 5 days we want 12 × 5 = 60 units. If 8 are in stock, order 60 − 8 = 52.
